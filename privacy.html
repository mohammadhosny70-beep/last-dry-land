Privacy Policy

Last Dry Land · Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Who we are

Last Dry Land ("the Game") is made by Astralabs (sole proprietor: Mohammad Hosny Awad), based in the United Arab Emirates ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy: cosmicwarriors.support@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

None. The Game is single-player and works offline. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

  • Your progress (islands won, stars, goals met, mode best scores, a match in progress, sound and other settings) is stored only on your device. We cannot see it.
  • The one-time "Full Game" unlock is processed entirely by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy. We never receive your payment details, and the Game does not send purchase records to us.

3. What we do not collect

  • Name, email address, phone number or government ID.
  • Payment card details (handled entirely by Google).
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs or usage analytics.

4. Data sharing

We do not share, sell, rent or trade any data, because we do not collect any.

5. Deleting your data

All game data lives on your device. Uninstalling the Game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, erases it.

6. Children

The Game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds anything that handles personal data, we will update this policy before releasing it. The date at the top shows the latest change.
